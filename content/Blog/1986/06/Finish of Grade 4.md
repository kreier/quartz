## Deutschunterricht

**"The Lantern Man"**, Maxim Gorky
- In what way does he succeed in breaking the boys of their habit of throwing stones at lanterns?

_(Note: "abgewöhnen" is translated here as "breaking a habit")_

**"Little Flame" (Flämmchen)**, Günter Feustel

Explain why we should not forget this story of Little Flame!

She should serve as a role model to us because she stood up for what she firmly believed in, and we too should take the necessary actions to stand up for what we believe in—for instance, by overcoming fear.
