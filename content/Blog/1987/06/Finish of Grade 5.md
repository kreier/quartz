### A few quick notes on the literary titles:

- **"Flämmchen"**: Literally means "Little Flame". Since it functions as a proper name/character in the book, keeping it in German or using "Little Flame" both work well depending on your target audience.
    
- **"Ede und Unku"**: This is a very well-known classic of German children's literature (about the friendship between a Berlin boy and a Sinti girl during the Weimar Republic). The names are proper nouns, so they remain unchanged.
    
- **"Die Kreuzotter"**: Literally the European adder or common viper.
