This part in the north north has great mountains and roads through it. Many tourists take the Ha Giang loop through this area. Done it twice.
Ha Giang itself is 6 hours from Hanoi, you might take a night bus. Or do a crazy drive through the rain as I did with Minh on November 2rd, 2025. 6 hours to Ha Giang, but another 6 hours through the dark, cold, fog, mountain to Long Cu, the northernmost point of Vietnam. No rest or food for us, only the bike!
