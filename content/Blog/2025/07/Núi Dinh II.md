I was a little unhappy that we did not finish the <span style="color:red">red route</span> completely in November 2023 with [[Núi Dinh I]]. What bothered me that it is technically **just a daytrip**. Why not get up early and jump on the bike and finish it? Well, it took me 1.5 years to finally get up early and jump on the bike! Eventually I finished the whole loop in less than 4 hours. It is not that tough. I started 7:00 from Saigon with my bike. By 14:46 I had finished the 15 kilometer hike.
