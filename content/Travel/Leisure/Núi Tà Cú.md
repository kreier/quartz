The trail has various levels of quality. In sight of the cable car at lower elevations you have well maintained stone stairs. Further into the forest many parts have been swept away by rain. And the entrance to the final part behind the largest reclining Buddha statue in south east Asia is not obvious to find.

## Trail details
To enter the TTC tourist area you have to buy a ticket for 50k VND. The parks, waterways, bridges and garden area is well maintained.

### Part 1 - From park entrance to cable car station
To enter the park you need a ticked for 50k VND. The park area is nice decorated and well maintained by the TTC corporation. You can spent some time in this lower section. Some 200 meters from the cable car station is the start of the trail up to the mountain station of the cable car.
