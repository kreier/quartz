The trail has various levels of quality. In sight of the cable car at lower elevations you have well maintained stone stairs. Further into the forrest many parts have been swept away by rain. And the entrance to the final part behind the largest reclining Buddha statue in south east Asia is not obvious to find.

## Trail details
To enter the TTC tourist area you have to buy a ticket for 50k VND. The parks, waterways, bridges and garden area is well maintained.
