## Núi lửa Sok LU
You can see [this mountain](https://maps.app.goo.gl/FkWgxEr7B2cMYjdz6) from the Tri An Lake, but it's actually not a hiking spot. The nearby town is already at 180 meter elevation, and you can drive through banana fields to get closer and a height of 280 meters. The peak is a little more than 400 meter. A farmer told me that you have a narrow road up that is best 7:00 am to 8:00 am to avoid oncoming traffic since you can't pass. I could not find this trail on satelite pictures or the Strava heat map. Probably best to find another goal. Núi Chứa Chan is not far away! I check it out on 2026/03/11.
