## Mountains in Vũng Tàu 

- Nghinh Phong, [Núi Nhỏ](https://vi.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%BAi_Nh%E1%BB%8F), **138** m, [Wikipedia - Christ of Vũng Tàu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ_of_V%C5%A9ng_T%C3%A0u), its more about the largest Christian statue across Asia
- Núi Tao Phùng, **176** m, [Hải đăng Vũng Tàu](https://vi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hải_đăng_Vũng_Tàu) (Vung Tao lighthouse) - closed during the night, good view over to the statue of Christ
- Núi Tương Kỳ, **245** m, [Núi Lơn](https://vi.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%BAi_L%E1%BB%9Bn), but now has the Du Lịch Hồ Mây with **400k entrance**. You can also take the cable care there, some 450k
- [[Núi Vũng Maý]], **235** m, nice round trip of 4 km in less than 2 hours: [link to AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/vietnam/ba-r-a-vung-tau/vung-may-loop) with heightmap
